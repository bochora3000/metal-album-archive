About the Metal Album Archive

Metal Album Archive is an independent listening and discovery project for heavy metal fans. Explore classic and modern metal records through 30 complete rankings, with 5,408 album entries spanning decades, subgenres and annual best-of lists. Discover essential albums in death metal, black metal, thrash metal, doom metal, traditional heavy metal and beyond.

Search for a band or album, choose a ranking, and mark records as listened to. Your progress stays in your browser and can be exported for backup.

Where the rankings come from

  • Encyclopaedia Metallum (Metal Archives) — community-voted decade rankings for the 1980s, 1990s, 2000s and 2010s.
  • Martin Popoff — The Top 500 Heavy Metal Albums of All Time.
  • Decibel Magazine — genre-specific, old-school and decade rankings.
  • Terrorizer Magazine — editorial and reader rankings, including its Secret History series.
  • Kerrang! — the 50 heaviest albums of all time.
  • Annual Album of the Year lists — supplied rankings from 2016–2025, excluding 2020; the 2024 list is marked unofficial.

This is an unofficial archive, not affiliated with the original publishers. Rankings reflect their respective sources, not a single editorial verdict. Repeated albums and original ranking positions are retained. Some source details have not been independently verified.
